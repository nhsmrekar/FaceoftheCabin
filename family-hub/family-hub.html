Display
Google OAuth
Set by the host this page was served from (see GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID in .env on the M920q) — not editable per device. Every device loading the Family Hub from this host gets the same value automatically.
Create a Web application client at console.cloud.google.com → APIs & Services → Credentials. Add as an authorized JavaScript origin (no redirect URI is used by this token flow). Keep the app in Testing and add smrekarfamilia@gmail.com as a test user.
Google Calendar
Paste a calendar email, URL, or the embed iframe — the email address will be extracted automatically. After saving, the field always shows the extracted email (e.g. smrekarfamilia@gmail.com).
Google Photos
Google no longer allows apps to browse arbitrary albums. Choose photos for this display with the supported Google Photos Picker. Shared photos.app.goo.gl links are not album IDs.
Shows near-real-time camera motion from the cabin on the Overview page. This reveals whether someone is physically at the cabin right now — full detail is the most useful day to day, but it's also the most informative to anyone else who happens to see this screen. "Activity only" drops the camera name/object/exact time; "Off" hides the widget entirely.
Parenting Schedule
Day 0 of the 14-day custody cycle. Chore tracking and the schedule widget count forward from this date.
Tap to toggle. Highlighted = Dad's days. Changing the anchor date to a new date starts a new schedule version from then forward — past days keep computing under whatever schedule was actually in effect. Changes take effect on Save.
Holidays & Vacations
A holiday date overrides the regular cycle for that one day — belongs wholly to Mom's or Dad's side regardless of the rotation.
Upcoming Holidays
Soonest first, left to right. Filter by parent, narrow the date range, or search by name.
Observations
Recorded days come from the daily custody log; scheduled days are computed from the active schedule version and any holiday overrides.
Theme
Access Log (admin only)
Visible only when signed in as an admin account. Logged locally in this browser — not yet synced across devices (see ROADMAP).
